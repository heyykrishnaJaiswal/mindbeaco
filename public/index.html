            Helplines — free and confidential

            Government-run services in India. Tap a number to call. If you are in immediate danger, call 112.

            Tele-MANAS

            14416

            Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. 24×7 tele-mental-health support in many languages. Also 1800-891-4416.

            Call 14416 1800-891-4416

            KIRAN

            1800-599-0019

            Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. 24×7 mental health rehabilitation helpline.

            Call KIRAN

            Emergency

            112

            National emergency number for police, ambulance and fire. Use it if you or someone else is in immediate danger.

            Call 112

            Childline

            Government helpline for anyone under 18 who needs care and protection. Free, 24×7.

            Call 1098

            NIMHANS

            080-46110007

            National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences, Bengaluru. Psychosocial support line.

            Call NIMHANS

            Numbers can change. Confirm on telemanas.mohfw.gov.in. Also reach out to your campus counselor, a doctor, or someone you trust. Outside India, contact your local crisis line.
